# Policy and governance

- No fake engagement, bought views/subscribers, deceptive metadata, or rights-uncleared assets.
- Human approval is ON by default.
- AI disclosure is evidence-driven; ordinary production assistance is not automatically tagged.
- Variation controls reject near-duplicate scripts.
- Advertiser pre-check uses LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH heuristic risk tiers and is not a monetization guarantee.
- Kill switch blocks new execution and publishing.


## External-video human review

- Automated checks can inspect only text/metadata available to the engine; they cannot verify the uploaded video's actual visuals or audio.
- The QC stage stores a disclosure suggestion and reason as a pre-fill hint only.
- The final disclosure_required value is never set from that suggestion. It is set only from the founder's explicit Yes/No review submission.
- The founder must explicitly confirm: "Maine poora video dekh liya hai aur ye script/brief se match karta hai." The confirmation is never pre-checked.
- Approval is blocked until the review confirmation and explicit disclosure answer have both been submitted.
