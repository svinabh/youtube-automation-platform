# Policy and governance

- No fake engagement, bought views/subscribers, deceptive metadata, or rights-uncleared assets.
- Human approval is ON by default.
- AI disclosure is evidence-driven: realistic synthetic/altered content that triggers disclosure is tagged; ordinary production assistance is not automatically tagged.
- Variation controls reject near-duplicate scripts.
- Advertiser pre-check flags risks but is not a monetization guarantee.
- Kill switch blocks new execution and publishing.
